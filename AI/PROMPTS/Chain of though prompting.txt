Question: A chemist has two acid solutions. Solution A is 20 percent acid and Solution B is 50 percent acid. He needs to create a 12-liter mixture that is 40 percent acid. How many liters of each solution should he use?

Answer:-
Let x be the liters of Solution A and y be the liters of Solution B.
Total volume constraint: x + y = 12. Therefore, x = 12 - y.
Total acid content constraint: 0.20x + 0.50y = 0.40 * 12.
Simplify the acid equation: 0.20x + 0.50y = 4.8.
Multiply the entire equation by 10 to remove decimals: 2x + 5y = 48.
Substitute x = 12 - y into the new equation: 2(12 - y) + 5y = 48.
Expand the brackets: 24 - 2y + 5y = 48.
Combine like terms: 24 + 3y = 48.
Subtract 24 from both sides: 3y = 24.
Solve for y: y = 8.
Calculate x: x = 12 - 8 = 4.
Answer: Use 4 liters of Solution A and 8 liters of Solution B.


Question: An investor puts a total of 10,000 dollars into two accounts. Account A earns 4.5 percent annual interest, and Account B earns 7 percent annual interest. After one year, the total interest earned is 630 dollars. How much was invested in Account A?

Answer:
Let x be the amount in Account A and y be the amount in Account B.
Total investment: x + y = 10,000. Therefore, y = 10,000 - x.
Total interest equation: 0.045x + 0.07y = 630.
Multiply the entire equation by 1,000 to remove decimals: 45x + 70y = 630,000.
Substitute y: 45x + 70(10,000 - x) = 630,000.
Expand the brackets: 45x + 700,000 - 70x = 630,000.
Combine like terms: -25x + 700,000 = 630,000.
Subtract 700,000 from both sides: -25x = -70,000.
Divide by -25: x = 2,800.
Answer: The amount invested in Account A was 2,800 dollars.


Question: A food scientist is mixing two types of grain. Grain X contains 10 percent protein and Grain Y contains 18 percent protein. How many kilograms of Grain X must be mixed with Grain Y to create a 20-kilogram batch that contains 15.2 percent protein?

Step-by-Step Solution:
Let x be the kg of Grain X and y be the kg of Grain Y.
Total weight: x + y = 20. Therefore, y = 20 - x.
Protein content equation: 0.10x + 0.18y = 0.152 * 20.
Calculate the right side: 0.152 * 20 = 3.04.
Equation: 0.10x + 0.18y = 3.04.
Multiply the entire equation by 100 to remove decimals: 10x + 18y = 304.
Substitute y: 10x + 18(20 - x) = 304.
Expand the brackets: 10x + 360 - 18x = 304.
Combine like terms: -8x + 360 = 304.
Subtract 360 from both sides: -8x = -56.
Divide by -8: x = 7.
Answer: 7 kilograms of Grain X must be used.
